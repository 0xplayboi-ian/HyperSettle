# Colosseum Eternal Evaluation Rubric & HyperSettle Strategic Alignment

This document outlines the official evaluation dimensions for Colosseum Eternal and specifies how **HyperSettle** systematically addresses, proves, and scores at the highest percentile on each criterion.

---

## 1. Official Evaluation Dimensions

Founders applying to Colosseum Eternal are evaluated across six core dimensions:

1. **Founder + Market Fit:** Who is the team creating this product? Does the team have the right skill set and experience to succeed in the market they’re entering? Why are they interested in solving this problem?
2. **Insight:** Does the founding team have some unique insight based on their deep understanding of the problem space? Is there a new technology, or a new trend that creates an opportunity for their startup to succeed?
3. **Product + Execution:** How well does the product work? How does it stack up against the competition? How quickly is the team shipping product updates and addressing user feedback?
4. **Potential Market Size (TAM):** How big is the total addressable market for this project? Is it already large, or small but growing rapidly? What will be the impact of this project on the growth rate of their market?
5. **Founder Communication:** Are the founders clearly communicating the product vision and capable of growing their product’s user base?
6. **Viability:** Is there a scalable and sustainable business to be built around this project? At its core, Eternal is a startup competition, so this is critical. Make sure to allocate enough time on your presentation to give a holistic view of your startup.
